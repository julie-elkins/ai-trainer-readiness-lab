# Learner feedback

Complete this after the workshop. This form helps improve the next delivery.
It is not a grade, a credential decision, or proof that you can transfer the
workflow to real work.

Do not include your name, contact information, personal data, confidential
work information, or private learner conversations.

## Confidence before and after

Use a scale from 1 to 5:

- **1:** I cannot yet explain or perform this.
- **3:** I can do this with some support.
- **5:** I can explain and perform this independently in this practice setting.

Confidence before the workshop: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5

Confidence after the workshop: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5

The confidence change does not replace the rubric or teach-back evidence.
